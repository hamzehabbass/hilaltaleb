HAVE A PROPERTY TO SELL?

Give your property
a clear introduction.

Tell us about your land or home in Al-Danniyeh. Start with the basics and we’ll follow up about your inquiry directly.

BEFORE YOU SEND THE DETAILS

Information that helps us understand it

You do not need a complete listing to get started. Share what you know and we’ll help clarify what is needed next.

01 / LOCATION

Where is it?

Share the village or nearby area and, if possible, the property location.

02 / DETAILS

What type and size?

Tell us if it is land, a house, or an apartment, and include known area details.

03 / FOLLOW-UP

How can we reach you?

Leave a phone number and a convenient time, and we’ll get back to discuss next steps.

Clear details help both sides start a better conversation.

PREPARATION CHECKLIST

What to have ready

  • The area and a short description of the location.
  • The property type and approximate or documented area.
  • Your asking price, if you have one, or let us know you prefer to discuss it.
  • Recent, clear property photos, if available.
  • Any documents or details you want a qualified professional to review before a sale.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

It starts with your inquiry

Sending an inquiry does not publish the property automatically. We’ll contact you to review the details and confirm what may be shown before anything is posted.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect

Will my property appear as soon as I submit the form?

No. The office will contact you first to review the information and get your approval for the details to be published.

Do I need to set a price?

You can include an asking price if you have one. If not, send your inquiry and discuss the details with us directly.

Can I send photos later?

Yes. Start with the basic information, then contact us to share photos or additional details.
