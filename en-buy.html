From search to viewing

Three steps to organize your search

Start with the details that matter to you, ask about each option, and get in touch to plan what comes next.

01 / DEFINE

What do you need?

Note the property type, preferred area, approximate size, and budget that work for you.

02 / COMPARE

Ask for the details

Ask about the location, price, area, and condition. Confirm what matters to you before a viewing.

03 / CONNECT

Plan a viewing

Once an option feels right, contact us to discuss the details and arrange a site visit.

BUYER CHECKLIST

Useful questions to compare

This checklist helps you gather information. It is not a substitute for professional legal or technical review.

  • What is the exact location and what are the property boundaries?
  • What is the documented area, and how was it measured?
  • What is the asking price, and what does it include?
  • What is the property's condition and any known repair work?
  • Which documents and details should a qualified local professional review before you commit?

VERIFIED DETAILS

Ask, confirm, then decide

Details vary by property. Request relevant answers and documents, and consult a qualified lawyer or local expert for any review needed before reaching an agreement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch

How do I ask about a specific property?

Open the properties page and use the inquiry link on a listing. You can also call or message us on WhatsApp.

Are listed prices and areas final?

Listings show the details entered by the office. Contact us to confirm price, area, and availability before relying on them.

Can I ask about a property that is not listed?

Tell us the property type and area you have in mind. We’ll review your inquiry and follow up if relevant information becomes available.
